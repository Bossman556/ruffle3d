About Ruffle3D

Short: Ruffle3D is CAD for product design. It does parametric solid modelling and free-form NURBS surfacing in one offline application, free for everyone.

Long: Ruffle3D combines history-based parametric solid modelling with free-form Class-A style surfacing. That includes CV curves and surfaces, surface fillets, trims, and curvature, zebra and isophote evaluation. Rendering and engineering drawings are built in too. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, keeps every design on the user's own computer, and exports STEP, STL and 3MF. Ruffle3D is free for everyone, including commercial use.
