About Ruffle3D

Professional CAD has become a subscription that costs as much as a used car over a few years. We think designers deserve a capable tool that costs nothing and keeps their work on their own computer. So Ruffle3D is free, for everyone.

What we're building

Ruffle3D is a CAD application for product design. It brings parametric solid modelling and free-form NURBS surfacing together, which usually means buying two products. Renders and engineering drawings are part of the same app. It's meant for industrial designers, mechanical engineers, makers and small studios: people who design physical things and need precise geometry they can manufacture.

How it's built

Ruffle3D's geometry comes from OpenCascade, an industrial boundary-representation kernel that has been developed for more than thirty years. We run it as WebAssembly in a background thread, so the interface stays responsive while the model rebuilds. On top of it we wrote our own sketch constraint solver, region detection, parametric rebuild engine, NURBS editing and surface evaluation tools. Rendering uses three.js. The full list of open-source components and their licences is on the open-source notices page.

What we believe

Your designs are yours

Ruffle3D runs on your computer. There is no account to create and no cloud storage of your projects. Your designs don't pass through our servers. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Free, with nothing locked

Ruffle3D is free for personal, educational and commercial use. There's no paid tier, so nothing is held back: surfacing, rendering, drawings, simulation and CAM are there for everyone.

Open file formats

You can always get your work out. STEP, STL, 3MF, GLB and OBJ export are included for everyone, and project files are plain JSON.

Honest about limits

Ruffle3D doesn't do everything yet. We publish what's missing in the release notes so you can decide whether it fits your work.
