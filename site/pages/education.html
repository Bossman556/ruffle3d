Ruffle3D for education

Ruffle3D is free for students, teachers and schools, as it is for everyone. It's the same software professionals use, with every feature included.

Students

Download Ruffle3D and use it for coursework, personal projects and your portfolio. You don't need to register or verify anything.

Teachers

Use Ruffle3D in lessons and set assignments with it. Students can run it from a USB stick or in the browser, and nothing needs installing on school machines.

Schools and labs

The download package runs without administrator rights. For computer labs or networks without internet access, contact us and we can help you deploy it.

What's covered

Everything. Coursework, theses, competitions, teaching, research with or without an industry partner, and the start-up you found after graduating: Ruffle3D is free for all of it, and you own everything you make.
