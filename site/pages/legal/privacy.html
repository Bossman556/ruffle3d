This policy explains what personal data {{company.legalName}} ("we") processes, and why. We are the data controller for the data described here.

The short version: the Ruffle3D application doesn't collect, send or store any of your data on our systems. Your designs stay on your device. We only handle personal data when you email us.

1. The Ruffle3D application

Ruffle3D runs on your device. Your projects, versions, thumbnails and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) for the address the app runs on. They never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. If you use the hosted version at this website's /app/ address, our web host receives the same basic request data as for any web page (see section 2), but not your designs.

2. This website

The website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies, and doesn't load fonts or scripts from third parties. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs to run and secure the site: IP address, date and time, the page requested and your browser's user agent. We use these logs only to operate and protect the website, and they are deleted within 30 days.

3. Downloads

Ruffle3D is free and needs no account, so downloading it doesn't involve any personal data beyond the standard server logs described above.

4. Email and support

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply, and we keep the conversation for up to two years after it ends. If you send a project file, we use it only to resolve your request and delete it afterwards. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the business: our web host and our email provider. They process it on our instructions or as required by law. We don't sell personal data, or share it for advertising.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
