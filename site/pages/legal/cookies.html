This website

This website doesn't set any cookies. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and it doesn't load fonts or scripts from other companies. That's why there's no cookie banner.

The Ruffle3D application

The application uses your browser's local storage to work. This is strictly necessary storage, kept on your device and never sent to us:

StorageNamePurposeKept
IndexedDBruffle3dYour projects, versions and thumbnailsUntil you delete them
localStorageruffle3d.prefsPreferences such as theme, accent colour and mouse presetUntil you clear them
localStorageruffle3d.lastProject, ruffle3d.designTabReopening your last project and toolbar tabUntil you clear them
Cache storageruffle3d-*Offline copy of the app files when installed from the browserUntil the next update

Clearing your browser's site data for Ruffle3D's address removes all of this, including your projects. Download project files first if you want to keep them.
