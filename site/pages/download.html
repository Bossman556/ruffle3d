Or install from your browser

In Chrome or Edge, open Ruffle3D and choose Install Ruffle3D from the address bar or the app's Project menu. You get a desktop window and an app icon. After the first launch it keeps working without an internet connection.

System requirements

Operating systemWindows 10 or 11 · macOS 12 Monterey or newer · Linux (x64 or ARM64) with a desktop environment
Browser engineBuilt into the desktop app. The portable packages and the web version use Chrome or Edge 110+, Firefox 115+, or Safari 16.4+.
GraphicsWebGL 2 capable GPU; integrated graphics are fine for most parts
Memory4 GB minimum, 8 GB recommended for large assemblies
Disk spaceAbout 250 MB for the desktop app (30 MB for the portable package), plus your projects
InputA mouse with a scroll wheel is recommended; trackpads work
Portable packages (macOS and Linux)Node.js 18+ or Python 3.8+

Verify the download
