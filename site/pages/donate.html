Before you send

  • Check the address in your wallet before you confirm. Crypto payments can't be reversed, and coins sent to a wrong address or on the wrong network can't be recovered.
  • The addresses on this page are the only ones Ruffle3D uses. We will never ask you for a donation by email or direct message.
  • Donations are voluntary gifts to the project. They don't buy a licence, support or any service, and they aren't tax-deductible.

Other ways to help

  • Star the project on GitHub and tell people who design things about it.
  • Report bugs and suggest features.
  • Contribute code, tests or documentation.
